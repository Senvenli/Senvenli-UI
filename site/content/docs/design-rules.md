These rules are built into the components. They are worth following in your own screens too.

## Color

- **Soft black and white.** Surfaces are `#fcfcfb` and `#0c0c0b`, never pure white or pure black.
- **One brand color does the talking.** Primary actions, selection, focus and links use it; the rest stays neutral.
- **Status colors mean status.** Green for success, amber for warning, red for danger, blue for information. Never for decoration.
- **No gradients, no glows.**

## Surfaces

- **Flat.** Surfaces separate by tone and space, not by borders at rest. Shadows are reserved for things that float: menus, popovers, dialogs.

## Focus

- **No focus rings.** Keyboard focus turns the control's own border the focus color, 2px, inside its edge. Nothing grows or shifts.

## Type

- **Sentence case** everywhere: buttons, headings, labels. No uppercase or tracked labels.
- **Two weights:** regular and medium.
- **Words that say what happens.** Buttons are verbs ("Save changes"); labels are nouns; errors say what to do (`Enter an email like name@example.com.`).
